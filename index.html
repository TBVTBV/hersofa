A small railway town in the forests of Småland, seen from the air in 1951.

Älmhult from the air, 1951. AB Flygtrafik, Kulturparken Småland, public domain

Ingvar Kamprad.

Born in 1926 in Småland. From the age of six he grows up on Elmtaryd, a farm near the village of Agunnaryd.

Ingvar and his sister Kerstin, around 1932. Family photo

At five, he sells matches.

He buys them in bulk in Stockholm and sells them one by one to the neighbours, at a profit.

Then fish, Christmas tree decorations, seeds, pens and pencils.

Ingvar Kamprad in the 1940s. Family photo
